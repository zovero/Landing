Shipping
Policy

We keep delivery straightforward, trackable, and carefully handled from our facility to your doorstep.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Order processing

Orders are usually processed within 1–3 business days after payment confirmation. Made-to-order, installation, or temporarily unavailable items may require additional time; we will communicate any expected delay.

2. Delivery timelines

Typical delivery within India is 3–10 business days after dispatch, depending on destination and courier availability. Remote locations, public holidays, weather, and other events outside our control may extend these estimates. Delivery estimates are not guarantees.

3. Charges and tracking

Shipping charges, if applicable, are shown before you confirm your order. Once dispatched, tracking information will be shared through the contact details provided at checkout. Please ensure your address and phone number are accurate.

4. Delivery issues

Inspect the package when it arrives. If it is visibly damaged, record the condition and contact us promptly. For a missing, delayed, or incorrect shipment, email support@zovero.in with your order details so we can investigate with the courier.

5. International shipping

International delivery is available only when confirmed by our team. Customs duties, import taxes, and local clearance charges are the recipient’s responsibility unless stated otherwise.
