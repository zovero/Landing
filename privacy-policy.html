Policy

Your trust matters. This policy explains how Zovero collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit our website or contact us.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, such as your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, vehicle details, and the contents of your enquiry. We may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, device information, and pages visited.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, process and deliver orders, provide installation or support, improve our products and website, prevent fraud, and meet legal or operational requirements. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing and security

Information may be shared only with service providers who help us operate the business, such as payment, delivery, hosting, and customer-support providers, or where required by law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

4. Cookies and your choices

Our website may use essential cookies or similar technologies for functionality and basic analytics. You can adjust cookie settings in your browser. You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information by contacting support@zovero.in.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Zovero at support@zovero.in. We may update this policy from time to time by posting a revised version on this page.
